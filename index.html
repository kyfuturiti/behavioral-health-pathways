Why this matters now

Kentucky's behavioral health workforce is in critical demand.

Mental health and substance use challenges have stretched our communities thin — and our workforce hasn't kept up. Whether you want to support people with disabilities, work in addiction recovery, become a counselor, or run a treatment program, Kentucky needs you.
